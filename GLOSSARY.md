# Recherche de maison

A watch over French real-estate listings that tracks the houses a family could buy and tells the family what changed.

## Language

### Houses and listings

**Bien**:
A house the veille tracks, with one number, one price and one or more Annonces.
_Avoid_: maison suivie, propriété

**Annonce**:
One listing of a Bien on one real-estate site, identified by its URL.
_Avoid_: lien, offre

**Verdict**:
The fit of a Bien with the search criteria: top, presque or hors.
_Avoid_: score, note

### Watch and report

**Passage**:
One execution of the veille, which reads the sources and updates the Biens.
_Avoid_: run, exécution

**Récapitulatif**:
The email sent to the family after a Passage, listing the Nouvelles maisons, Baisses de prix and sold or withdrawn Biens that no earlier Récapitulatif contained.
_Avoid_: mail, rapport, résumé, newsletter

**Nouvelle maison**:
A Bien added to the tracking since the last Récapitulatif.
_Avoid_: nouvelle annonce, nouveauté

**Baisse de prix**:
A lower price recorded since the last Récapitulatif on an active Bien that the family has not marked « pas intéressés ».
_Avoid_: baisse, réduction
